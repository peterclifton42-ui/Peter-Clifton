## SECTION 1: PROFITABILITY METRICS

### 1. RETURN ON AVERAGE ASSETS (ROAA)

**What It Measures:**
Net income as a percentage of average total assets. Shows how efficiently the bank converts assets into profit.

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Strong: >1.2%
- Satisfactory: 0.9-1.2%
- Needs Improvement: <0.9%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Below 1.0%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Bank is underperforming peers, likely feeling revenue pressure
- **Declining trend:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Creates urgency for solutions
- **Above 1.2%:** LOW-MEDIUM - They're doing well but may want to maintain edge

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
Low ROAA indicates the bank is struggling to generate profits from their asset base. This opens doors for:
- Revenue enhancement solutions
- Cost reduction tools  
- Efficiency improvements
- Better asset mix optimization

**Conversation Starter:**
"Your ROAA of 0.85% is below the peer median of 1.1%. That gap represents $X million in lost profitability. Our clients typically see [X] basis points improvement through better deposit pricing and efficiency tools."

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **Deposit Pricing & Profitability Suite** - Optimize funding costs to improve NIM
2. **Efficiency & Expense Analytics** - Identify cost reduction opportunities
3. **Commercial Banking Profitability** - Enhance relationship returns
4. **Branch Network Optimization** - Right-size physical footprint

### 2. RETURN ON AVERAGE EQUITY (ROAE)

**What It Measures:**
Net income as a percentage of average shareholder equity. Shows returns delivered to shareholders.

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Strong: >12%
- Satisfactory: 8-12%
- Needs Improvement: <8%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Below 8%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Pressure from shareholders/board for improvement
- **Below peer average:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Competitive disadvantage
- **Strong but declining:** MEDIUM - Proactive buyers wanting to maintain leadership

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
Banks with low ROAE face:
- Shareholder pressure
- Difficulty raising capital
- Risk of being acquisition targets
- Board-level urgency for improvement

This creates C-suite involvement in solution purchases.

**Conversation Starter:**
"At 7.8% ROAE, you're likely facing shareholder questions. Top quartile performers at 13-15% ROAE have one thing in common: they use data-driven pricing and profitability analytics. What's holding you back from that level?"

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **Comprehensive Profitability Platform** - End-to-end profit optimization
2. **Deposit Pricing Suite** - Largest impact on ROE through NIM expansion
3. **Fee Income Analytics** - Identify non-interest income opportunities

**Opportunity Score Matrix:**
- <7%: HIGH (urgent board-level pressure)
- 7-9%: MEDIUM-HIGH (below peer, needs action)
- 9-11%: MEDIUM (room for improvement)
- >11%: LOW-MEDIUM (defensive/maintain position)

### 3. RETURN ON TANGIBLE COMMON EQUITY (ROTCE)

**What It Measures:**
Net income relative to tangible equity (excluding goodwill). Shows core operating profitability.

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Strong: >15%
- Satisfactory: 10-15%
- Needs Improvement: <10%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Below 10%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Especially important for M&A-active banks
- **Large gap between ROAE and ROTCE:** HIGH - Indicates acquisition drag
- **Strong ROTCE:** MEDIUM - They understand data-driven management

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
ROTCE is the "real" profitability metric that strips out M&A accounting. Banks focused on ROTCE are:
- Sophisticated buyers
- Likely using analytics already
- Focused on operational excellence
- Good candidates for premium solutions

**Conversation Starter:**
"Your 11.5% ROTCE vs. 8.9% ROAE shows goodwill drag from acquisitions. Post-M&A banks that deploy our integration and synergy tools typically see [X] bps ROTCE improvement in [Y] months."

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **M&A Integration Platform** - Realize synergies faster
2. **Profitability Analytics** - Focus on tangible returns
3. **Efficiency Tools** - Improve operating leverage

### 4. NET INTEREST MARGIN (NIM)

**What It Measures:**
Net interest income as a percentage of earning assets. The #1 driver of bank profitability.

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Strong: >3.5%
- Satisfactory: 3.0-3.5%
- Compressed: <3.0%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Below 3.0%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Severe margin pressure, urgent need
- **Declining trend:** HIGH - Even if absolute level is ok, trend creates urgency
- **Below peer by 20+ bps:** HIGH - Competitive disadvantage
- **Above 3.5%:** MEDIUM - Want to maintain, fear complacency

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
NIM is THE metric bank CEOs care about most. When NIM is under pressure:
- It's a board-level topic
- Creates urgency for solutions
- Executives will pay for tools that help
- Opens doors to C-suite meetings

Every 1 basis point of NIM = $0.1 million in annual revenue per $1B of earning assets (e.g. 1 bp on $50B = $5M).

**Conversation Starter:**
"Your NIM of 2.85% is 45 basis points below peers. On your $50B balance sheet, that's $225 million in lost annual revenue. Our Deposit Pricing Suite clients typically see [X] bps NIM improvement within [Y] months. Can we show you how?"

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **Deposit Pricing & Profitability Suite** (PRIMARY) - Optimize funding costs
2. **Loan Pricing Analytics** - Improve asset yields  
3. **Balance Sheet Optimization** - Better asset/liability mix
4. **Rate Intelligence** - Competitive pricing insights

**This is typically the #1 HIGHEST OPPORTUNITY metric for Curinos sales**

### 5. EFFICIENCY RATIO

**What It Measures:**
Non-interest expense divided by total revenue. Lower is better (more efficient).

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Best-in-class: <50%
- Good: 50-55%
- Average: 55-60%
- Needs Improvement: >60%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Above 60%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Major cost problem
- **Above 55%:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Room for improvement
- **Increasing trend:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Losing operating leverage
- **Below 52%:** LOW - Already efficient

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
High efficiency ratios indicate:
- Bloated cost structure
- Too many branches
- Redundant technology
- Post-merger integration issues
- Overstaffing

These banks NEED cost analytics tools.

**Conversation Starter:**
"At 58.5% efficiency ratio, you're spending $58.50 for every $100 of revenue. Peer leaders at 52-54% have measurable advantages: branch productivity analytics, expense benchmarking, and smart staffing models. What's your plan to close that 400-600 bps gap?"

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **Efficiency & Expense Analytics** (PRIMARY)
2. **Branch Network Optimization** - Identify underperformers
3. **Staffing & Productivity Tools** - Right-size workforce
4. **Technology ROI Analysis** - Optimize vendor spending

## SECTION 2: CAPITAL & RISK METRICS

### 6. COMMON EQUITY TIER 1 (CET1) RATIO

**What It Measures:**
Highest quality capital as percentage of risk-weighted assets. Regulatory safety cushion.

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Regulatory minimum: 4.5% + buffers = ~7-8%
- Well-capitalized: >10%
- Fortress balance sheet: >12%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Below 9%:** LOW OPPORTUNITY - Capital constrained, not buying anything
- **9-11%:** MEDIUM - Normal range, some flexibility
- **Above 12%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Excess capital, looking to deploy or grow

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
Capital levels tell you their strategic posture:
- **Low capital:** Can't pursue growth, avoiding costs
- **Normal capital:** Selective buyers, need strong ROI
- **Excess capital:** Likely planning M&A or organic growth, will invest in tools

**Conversation Starter:**
"Your 12.8% CET1 suggests you have capital to deploy. Banks in your position typically face a choice: M&A or organic growth. Either way, our platforms help you optimize that deployment. Are you evaluating acquisitions or focusing on organic expansion?"

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **M&A Integration Suite** (if pursuing acquisitions)
2. **Commercial Banking Growth Tools** (if organic growth focus)
3. **Deposit Gathering Analytics** (if need to fund growth)

### 7. LOAN-TO-DEPOSIT RATIO (LDR)

**What It Measures:**
Total loans divided by total deposits. Measures funding dependency.

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Conservative: <70%
- Balanced: 70-85%
- Aggressive: >85%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Below 70%:** MEDIUM OPPORTUNITY - Excess deposits, need to deploy or reduce cost
- **70-85%:** LOW - Balanced, comfortable position
- **Above 85%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Funding pressure, need deposits badly

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
LDR indicates funding pressure or excess liquidity:
- **Low LDR:** "We have too many expensive deposits" → Deposit optimization tools
- **High LDR:** "We need deposits to fund loan growth" → Deposit gathering tools

**Conversation Starter (Low LDR):**
"Your 68% LDR means you're sitting on $15B+ in excess deposits. What's your strategy to either deploy those loans or reduce deposit costs? Our clients in similar positions cut funding costs [X] bps."

**Conversation Starter (High LDR):**
"At 92% LDR, you're borrowing from FHLB to fund loans. That's expensive vs. deposits. Our Deposit Intelligence platform helps banks identify which markets and products offer the best deposit gathering opportunities."

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
- **Low LDR:** Deposit Pricing Suite, Cost of Funds Optimization
- **High LDR:** Deposit Gathering Analytics, Market Intelligence

### 8. NON-PERFORMING LOAN (NPL) RATIO

**What It Measures:**
Loans 90+ days past due or non-accrual as % of total loans. Credit quality indicator.

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- Excellent: <0.5%
- Good: 0.5-1.0%
- Concerning: >1.0%

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Below 0.5%:** LOW OPPORTUNITY - Good credit quality
- **0.5-0.75%:** MEDIUM - Watchful management, open to risk tools
- **Above 1.0%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Credit stress, need better early warning

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
NPL levels indicate:
- Credit underwriting quality
- Collections effectiveness
- Economic stress in markets
- Need for risk analytics

**Conversation Starter:**
"Your NPL ratio increased from 0.6% to 0.9% over the past year. That's an early warning sign. Banks with robust credit risk analytics catch problem loans [X] quarters earlier. What early warning systems do you have in place?"

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **Credit Risk Analytics** (if available)
2. **Portfolio Monitoring Tools**
3. **Commercial Banking Intelligence** (for C&I portfolio risk)

## SECTION 3: GROWTH METRICS

Year-over-year percentage change in total assets. Proxy for overall growth.

**Typical Ranges:**
- Organic: 3-7% annually
- Aggressive organic: 8-12%
- M&A-driven: 15%+ (spikes)

**Sales Intelligence:**
- **Negative growth:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Shrinking, likely cost-focused (efficiency tools)
- **0-5%:** MEDIUM - Steady state, selective investments
- **6-12%:** HIGH OPPORTUNITY - Growing fast, need infrastructure
- **>15% (non-M&A):** HIGH - Hypergrowth, will buy tools to manage scale

**Why This Matters to Sales:**
Fast-growing banks have urgent needs:
- Systems are strained
- Manual processes breaking down
- Need to scale operations
- Will pay to avoid hiring more people

**Conversation Starter:**
"You've grown assets 18% annually for 3 years. That's impressive, but those growth rates stress manual processes. At what point do you automate deposit pricing, relationship profitability, and branch analytics instead of hiring more analysts?"

**Relevant Curinos Products:**
1. **Platform Solutions** (vs. point tools) for scaling
2. **Automation Tools** - Replace manual work
3. **Real-time Analytics** - Keep pace with growth

## SECTION 11: ROI QUANTIFICATION TOOLS

### Quick ROI Calculators for Common Scenarios:

**NIM Improvement:**
Improvement in bps × Total Assets in billions = Annual Revenue Impact
Example: 8 bps × $50B = $40M annually

**Efficiency Ratio Improvement:**
Improvement in percentage points × Total Revenue in billions = Annual Savings
Example: 2% × $2B revenue = $40M annually

**Deposit Retention:**
Deposit retention % × At-risk deposits × Cost savings vs. replacement
Example: 5% × $20B × 50 bps = $10M annually

**Branch Optimization:**
# Branches closed × Avg cost per branch - Revenue loss
Example: 30 branches × $400K - $2M revenue = $10M annually

**Loan Pricing:**
Yield improvement bps × Total loans
Example: 5 bps × $100B = $50M annually

Use these calculators IN THE MEETING to quantify value on the spot.
