### If the institution is a credit union

Catalogue V4 handles credit unions properly (and `score_v4.py` enforces the gates below mechanically), and the differences are load-bearing — get them wrong and the dashboard is worse than useless in the room:

- **Commercial Analyzer, Commercial Optimizer, Commercial Data, Treasury Management Fee Analyzer, Wealth Analyzer, Wealth Optimizer and Commercial Digital Analyzer are type-gated out** for credit unions. `score_v4.py` lists them in `dnrProducts[]` with the type gate as the reason; never move them back by hand.
- **Benchmark against the NCUA system, not bank peers** (K2.4, K6). ROAA vs system average; loan-to-share against the 78–84% system range; member value return trend. Note the inversion: for a credit union, sitting *below* the loan-to-share range is the pain signal (excess deposits), not above it.
- **Retail Deposit Optimizer unlocks at $30B** for credit unions, not the bank $50B (V4 C1 `deposit_optimizer_split`). Below $30B the scorer passes Deposit Optimizer Essentials instead. V3's $30B/$50B contradiction is resolved in V4: $30B everywhere for credit unions.
- **Language rule (I1 rule 12, Section J):** members, not customers. Frame as member value per dollar of cost — never margin extraction, never cross-sell. Curinos One is framed as "member relevance" (Amplero is retired in V4).
- Peers should be other credit unions plus the NCUA system average, not a set of regional banks.

**Recent CU mergers that break year-on-year comparisons** — a smooth-looking growth line here is an artefact, not performance. First Technology FCU absorbed Digital Federal (DCU) on 1 Jan 2026, roughly doubling it into the band; it is reported as the largest credit union merger on record. Suncoast announced an intent to merge with Launch CU in February 2026. Ent and Wings combined as a merger of equals on 1 Jan 2026 and the surviving brand is Wings. Any dashboard covering these must adjust for the merger or say plainly that it has not.
